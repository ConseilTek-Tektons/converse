# Converse operations knowledge

## Conversation routing

Rules evaluate inbox, customer tier, language, topic, availability, and service window.

Manual reassignment retains both the original decision and operator reason.

## Conversation privacy

Access is constrained by inbox membership, account relationship, and sensitive-topic labels.

Search and AI retrieval apply the same row-level visibility before returning content.

## Service-level timers

Timers use business calendars and pause only on configured customer-wait states.

Every pause and breach is derived from immutable message and assignment events.

## Product boundary

Converse does not include a global SMS or push-notification carrier network. External channels remain provider integrations with their own delivery constraints.
